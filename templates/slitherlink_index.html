📖 How to Play Slitherlink

  • Connect adjacent dots with vertical or horizontal lines to form a single, continuous loop.
  • The numbers indicate how many lines must surround that specific cell (0, 1, 2, or 3).
  • Empty cells can be surrounded by any number of lines.
  • The loop never crosses itself and never branches off.
  • Mouse Controls:
    - Click once: Draw a line (Blue).
    - Click twice: Mark an 'X' (Empty space).
    - Click three times: Clear the space.
